## Review Focus

1. **Specificity against the table's hover.** The current-row rule, `.ui-table tbody tr:is([aria-current]:not([aria-current="false"]), [aria-selected="true"]) > *`, outranks the hover rule, so the tint holds under the pointer. Pinned by `the current row keeps its tint under the pointer`.
2. **The avatar inside a square button** loses its circle and fills the button: `.ui-btn[data-shape="square"] > .ui-avatar`, in `chip.css`, after `button.css`. Pinned by `an avatar is a circle; inside a square button it fills the button`.
3. **The indeterminate meter stands still under reduced motion,** and the scoped comparison freezes animations before it snapshots. Pinned by `under reduced motion an indeterminate meter stands still` and both scoped tests.
4. **The overlay tests centre each opener before clicking.** The fixture grew above the menus, so an opener at the viewport's bottom edge would flip its menu above. The change is in the tests only.
5. **The site.** Every component stays in the home pile and on Components; nothing in the pile is covered at any tested width; the first screen still shows the Email field under reduced motion; table regions keep unique names (`Page part` and `Control` columns).
